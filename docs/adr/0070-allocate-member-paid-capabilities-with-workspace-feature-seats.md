# Allocate member paid capabilities with Workspace Feature Seats

Arclayer models member-assigned paid capabilities as Workspace-owned Entitlements with explicit Feature Seat allowances and revocable assignments to active memberships. A seat chooses who may consume the Workspace capability but grants no role, Workspace Permission, rollout inclusion, readiness, Credits, or health, and it never becomes a User-owned Entitlement or per-User rollout exception. The Workspace Owner always manages assignments; a non-Owner needs the independent `workspace:feature-seats:manage` permission, which allocates only existing capacity and grants no Plan, role, membership, or capability-use authority. Product API and paid-effect workers re-evaluate both the Workspace Entitlement and actor assignment, while customer copy describes assigning a usage place rather than granting a permission.
