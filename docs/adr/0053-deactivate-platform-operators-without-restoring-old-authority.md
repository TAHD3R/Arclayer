# Deactivate Platform Operators without restoring old authority

Routine Platform Operator offboarding removes Platform access rather than deleting or reversibly pausing the shared identity: one revision-bound apply barrier removes Platform Entry Eligibility, revokes Platform Sessions, end-dates effective roles and Delegable Platform Authority, and invalidates pending invitations sent by or addressed to the operator while retaining historical assignments, Audit attribution, and Accepted Platform Operations. Product identity and Workspace authority remain unchanged, the Instance Owner is excluded, and a former operator may return only through a new invitation, current role review, mandatory MFA satisfaction, activation, and a new Platform Session rather than restoring prior authority.
