# Recover Instance Ownership outside Platform RBAC

Instance Ownership Recovery is a deployment-configured break-glass authority outside Platform Console navigation, Platform APIs, Roles, Sessions, and Operator quorum: self-hosted deployments use offline or infrastructure-custodied recovery proof, while managed deployments use a reviewed support process whose custodians never become Owner or gain ordinary Platform access. After a declared notification/hold policy and complete revalidation, recovery either restores the verified same Owner with new MFA and revoked old Sessions or atomically replaces an unavailable Owner while preserving exactly one owner, deactivating former Platform access, revoking affected Sessions, rotating recovery material, and retaining tamper-evident evidence without storing raw proofs in general Audit.
