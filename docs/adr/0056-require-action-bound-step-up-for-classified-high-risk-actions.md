# Require action-bound step-up for classified high-risk Platform actions

Baseline Platform Session MFA remains sufficient for ordinary reads and low-risk saves, while each trusted action capability contract explicitly classifies high-risk final applies that require fresh Step-up Platform Verification, including ownership transfer, identity-provider authority changes, Platform privilege elevation, critical credential replacement, and later owning-domain actions with documented blast radius. The proof binds actor, Session, action and permission, target/scope, preview and catalog revisions, idempotency identity, and versioned freshness policy but never grants authority; final apply rechecks current permission and state, and Session, role, policy, preview, target, scope, or freshness drift invalidates the proof rather than reusing login MFA or challenging every routine mutation.
