# Configure Signals through typed domain policies

Each Platform Domain defines typed, versioned policies for the Operational Signals it may emit and exposes only product-approved fields such as meaningful thresholds, observation windows, severity mappings, and optional Operations Inbox admission. Authorized changes use a revision-bound recent-coverage preview and risk-tiered apply flow; mandatory security, Audit-completeness, or data-integrity policies may be non-disableable only with an exact invariant and plain-language explanation. SQL, arbitrary expressions, cross-domain joins, and generic rule builders are excluded, previews create no evidence, emitted Signals retain their policy revision, and policy changes affect existing items only through explicit source-domain reconciliation rather than deletion or historical rewrite.
