# Operate hosting, domains, and storage as site delivery

Hosting, domains, certificates, file storage, delivery policy, suspension, and restoration belong to one Hosting & Delivery Platform Domain whose product entry is the delivery-issue queue. Arclayer does not retain separate Domains and Storage settings categories or split storage into another top-level domain: these capabilities jointly determine whether a published site can be served and restored, while Project content and publication intent remain in the Product Application and historical analysis remains in Observability.
