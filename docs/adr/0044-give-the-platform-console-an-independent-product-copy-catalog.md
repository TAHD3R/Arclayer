# Give the Platform Console an independent product-copy catalog

`apps/platform` owns an independent `apps/platform/locales/{zh-CN,en-US}` catalog organized by real product capabilities, with stable semantic-intent keys such as `navigation:OPERATIONS_INBOX` rather than wording- or component-shaped identifiers. Legacy `NAV_GLOBAL_PLATFORM_*`, `NAV_SECTION_PLATFORM`, `SYSTEM_*`, `SETTINGS_*`, and other changed Product Application keys are deleted with their final consumers instead of imported or aliased; exact locale parity is enforced with cross-language fallback and default-English concealment disabled. Platform APIs return stable codes and typed parameters for frontend mapping, internal permission/Provider/state vocabulary stays in authorized diagnostics, and user-authored names, notes, reasons, Saved-view labels, and imported content remain verbatim rather than being machine-translated.
