# Operate Platform AI through an action queue

Platform AI capabilities belong to one Platform AI Operations Domain whose product entry is the cross-Workspace AI task queue, supported by model/provider, built-in assistant, action-policy, and runtime surfaces. Arclayer does not split Agents from AI infrastructure or retain the existing settings wall: operators need one path from failed or blocked work to its owning recovery action, while Workspace-authored context and approvals, Commerce state, and historical Observability remain with their existing authorities.
