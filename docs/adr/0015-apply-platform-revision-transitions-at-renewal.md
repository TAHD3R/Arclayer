# Apply Platform Revision Transitions at renewal

Platform-initiated **Subscription Revision Transitions** are **Renewal Transitions** by default: each Subscription keeps its source Revision through the current paid period and adopts the target Revision at its own next Provider-confirmed renewal boundary. A cohort may therefore transition across multiple dates. Immediate application is reserved for a separate, single-Workspace **Commercial Correction** backed by explicit source evidence and exact Provider, proration, Credit, Entitlement, and period consequences.

## Consequences

The billing Connector must support Subscription scheduling/change, cancellation, and proration preview before paid Renewal Transitions are available. Local paid state never moves ahead of Provider confirmation. Commerce has no immediate cohort toggle, and Provider-independent Subscriptions use the same durable boundary semantics through the local scheduler.
