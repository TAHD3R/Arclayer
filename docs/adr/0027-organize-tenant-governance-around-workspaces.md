# Organize Tenant Governance around Workspaces

Tenant Governance is one Platform Domain whose default operational surface is the Workspace governance queue, with Users and Projects as peer investigative views. Arclayer does not split those entities into separate Platform Domains or retain a Tenant Overview made of summary cards: most cross-tenant lifecycle, access, ownership, and commercial investigations converge on a Workspace, while exact Commerce, Hosting, Observability, and Workspace Administration actions remain with their owning domains and are reached through typed deep links.
